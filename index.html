Common Questions

Plainly answered.

What does SOLACE actually do?

SOLACE is a private coordination company for the Flathead Valley. We source, schedule, vet, and follow through on requests — from dinner reservations to vendor coordination to ongoing household management. We don't perform the work ourselves; we coordinate it with the right independent vendors on your behalf.

Who is SOLACE for?

Four kinds of relationships. Solace/Luxury: guests of high-end vacation rentals, directly or through their property managers. Solace/Care: families with a loved one in a Flathead Valley care facility. Solace/Home: homeowners with a second home in the valley. Direct to Solace: the whole-season relationship, by introduction only.

Where do you serve?

The Flathead Valley — Whitefish, Kalispell, Columbia Falls, Lakeside, Bigfork, and the Glacier National Park corridor.

How does pricing work?

You pay Solace a coordination fee, collected at booking before work begins. Fees are priced by complexity, not by the underlying service. All vendor costs, deliveries, materials, and gratuities are separate, pre-approved, and fully itemized. Solace does not accept undisclosed vendor commissions or referral fees.

How do I get started?

The simplest way is a short note here — a few words is enough. Guests of a partner vacation rental can also go through their property manager and the SOLACE app. Or call (406) 405-1266. We respond personally.
