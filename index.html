A Note from the Founder

Founder-led, because the details are where it matters.

Brittany Alcott is a Flathead Valley local, a mother, and the founder personally involved from the first inquiry through the planning process. SOLACE is not built around overpromising — it is built around listening carefully, coordinating thoughtfully, and helping the right people come together at the right time.

The families, guests, and second-home owners who come to SOLACE tend to need the same things: clear communication, practical planning, and care that still feels grounded. That is what a founder-led arrangement makes possible — and why SOLACE stays small enough to offer it well.
