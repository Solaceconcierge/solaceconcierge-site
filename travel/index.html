A Few Important Notes

A few things worth saying plainly.

Who provides what. Travel advisory services on this page are provided by Brittany Alcott, an independent travel advisor of Fora Travel. Local concierge and household coordination services in the Flathead Valley are provided separately by Solace Concierge LLC.

Travel reservations follow the applicable booking agency and supplier terms. Rates, deposits, and cancellation terms are set by each hotel and supplier and confirmed before anything is booked.

Travel advisory services may earn commissions on eligible bookings. Any separate planning or service fee will be disclosed and agreed upon before work begins. Reservation payments, deposits, changes and cancellations follow the terms provided for the specific booking. Solace’s local coordination fees and pre-funded vendor billing apply only to local concierge services, not to travel reservations.

Preferred-program benefits vary by property, program, and booking eligibility. They are extended at the hotel’s discretion and are never guaranteed.

Dining requests are confirmed with you before anything is booked. Any reservation fee, deposit, cancellation terms, or minimum spend set by the restaurant or booking partner is shared in advance. VIP Dining is available only at participating restaurants in select cities. VIP Dining reservations are made through our booking partner, PRIMA, under its own terms.

Solace’s local services remain rooted in the Flathead Valley. For local requests, visit Solace/Luxury, Solace/Home or Solace/Care.
