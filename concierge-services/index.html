What to prepare before your Whitefish arrival.

  1. Share your arrival and departure dates, property location, party size, and any access instructions through your host.
  2. Identify what you would like arranged: provisions, dining, transport, celebrations, or activities.
  3. Include preferences, dietary needs, ages where relevant, and a proposed budget.
  4. Allow time for availability checks. Confirm the written scope and costs before any booking or purchase.

For vacation rentals, your property manager remains the point of entry. SOLACE coordinates the agreed guest details alongside their team.

Questions about concierge services in Whitefish.

What does a concierge in Whitefish coordinate?

SOLACE coordinates arrival preparation, grocery and welcome arrangements, restaurant reservations, private-chef bookings, transportation arrangements, celebrations, and personalized itineraries. Independent providers perform the underlying services, and availability is confirmed for each request.

Who is SOLACE for?

SOLACE serves vacation-rental guests through their property managers, second-home owners, and Flathead Valley households. Solace/Luxury supports guest arrangements, while Solace/Home provides household and vendor coordination.

Does SOLACE replace a property manager?

No. SOLACE does not manage rental listings, reservations for accommodation, rent, or leases. We coordinate guest arrangements and household details alongside your property manager and other providers.

How much do guest concierge requests cost?

Solace/Luxury coordination fees are $225 for Simple, $450 for Moderate, $750 for Complex, and from $1,250 for Signature. Vendor services, purchases, delivery, and gratuities are separate and approved in advance. The final scope and quote are confirmed before work begins.

How far in advance should requests be made?

Standard Luxury requests require at least 48 hours’ notice. Requests within 24 hours may be accepted when available with a $250 rush surcharge; complex same-day requests carry a $500 surcharge. More notice is helpful for multi-day stays and requests involving several providers.

Where does SOLACE serve?

SOLACE serves Whitefish, Kalispell, Columbia Falls, Bigfork, Lakeside, and the Glacier National Park corridor in Montana. Service availability depends on the location, dates, scope, and participating providers.

How do I start?

Vacation-rental guests should ask their property manager to arrange a SOLACE request. Property managers and homeowners can email contact@solaceconcierge.com or call (406) 405-1266 to discuss scope and availability.
