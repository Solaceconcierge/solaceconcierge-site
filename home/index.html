What Solace/Home Is

A home that receives you. Not one that requires you.

For second-home owners and Flathead Valley households who'd rather not be the project manager of their own property. SOLACE/Home is private home coordination — vendor coordination, home readiness, seasonal details, arrival preparation, and the follow-through that keeps a home running while you live the rest of your life.

Solace/Home is not property management. It is the coordination of the loose ends your property manager, cleaner, or vendor doesn't handle — one point of contact for all of it.

How It Works

A standing arrangement. A schedule. A single point of contact.

  • Getting Started

    We begin with a walk-through of your home, a conversation about your trades, and an intake of how you like things kept — then assemble the standing vendor list and the seasonal calendar. For owners who want the home fully guest-ready, Solace/Home Setup handles the whole project end to end.

  • The Monthly Cadence

    Four household coordination requests each month, on whatever schedule the home asks for. A recurring cleaner. A seasonal task. A vendor confirmation. Anything beyond the four is arranged as needed.

  • One Point of Contact

    You call SOLACE — never the seven different vendors that keep your property running. We hold the relationships, we make the schedule, we follow up on the work.

  • Pass-Through Billing

    SOLACE charges the monthly retainer. Vendor costs, materials, and labor are quoted before each engagement and billed transparently to the homeowner on the same invoice. We do not mark up trades.

  • The SOLACE App

    A private SOLACE app for ongoing requests, vendor confirmations, and a clean view of your standing schedule — everything in one place, always within reach.

A Few Important Notes

A few things worth saying plainly.

SOLACE/Home is a coordination service — we source, schedule, vet, and oversee. The hands-on work is performed by licensed and insured Flathead Valley trades on the homeowner's behalf. We do not perform repair, cleaning, or maintenance work directly.

Pricing is for SOLACE coordination only. The cost of materials, labor, parts, and any vendor invoices are paid by the homeowner — quoted in advance, billed transparently, never marked up.

Subscriptions are month-to-month with a 30-day cancellation notice. Geographic coverage: Whitefish, Kalispell, Columbia Falls, Lakeside, Bigfork, and the Glacier corridor.
