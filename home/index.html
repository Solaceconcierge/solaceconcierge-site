Services

End-to-end property care.

From seasonal preparation to day-of arrival setup, Solace Home handles every layer of estate management — with the same discretion and attention we bring to every client relationship.

01 · Property Oversight

Routine Check-Ins

Regular walkthroughs of the property — interior and exterior — with photo documentation and issue reporting. You see everything. You don't have to be there.

02 · Arrival Coordination

Arrival-Ready Turnover

Pre-arrival preparation, stocking, cleaning coordination, and property setup so guests or owners arrive to a home that is perfectly prepared.

03 · Vendor Management

Trusted Trade Coordination

Scheduling and oversight of contractors, cleaners, landscapers, and maintenance crews. We use only vetted vendors from the Solace partner network.

04 · Seasonal Prep

Seasonal Transitions

Full seasonal preparation for Montana's demanding climate — winterization, spring opening, storm prep, and system checks before and after each season.

05 · Emergency Response

On-Call Issue Response

When something requires immediate attention, we respond. Water, heat, security, access — we have trusted vendors on standby for urgent situations.

06 · Guest Services

Guest Experience Support

Concierge support for your rental guests — local recommendations, logistics coordination, and a direct line so they feel taken care of throughout their stay.
