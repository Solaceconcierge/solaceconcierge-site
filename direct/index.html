How It Works

A single relationship, year over year.

  • The Introduction

    Direct to Solace is by introduction only — through an existing relationship, an existing client, or a quiet inquiry that becomes a first conversation. We do not run an open intake. We meet, we listen, we either fit or we don't.

  • The Retainer

    A single monthly retainer covers the household across the year. No per-request accounting, no hourly billing, no escalating tiers. Vendor costs, materials, and gratuities pass through transparently — never marked up.

  • Family Inclusion

    Children under eighteen living in the household are included by default. Adult family at the same address, or independently, are coordinated as a quiet add-on. Out-of-region travel during the engagement year is handled on a best-effort basis, with policy formalized after the first twelve months.

  • The Term

    Twelve-month initial term. Sixty-day termination notice after the initial term. Within the initial term, early termination carries a fifty-percent remainder commitment. After Year One the relationship auto-renews unless either party closes it.

  • The Line

    A 24-hour direct line to SOLACE. Not a switchboard. Not an inbox. A relationship — answered.

A Few Important Notes

The plain things, said plainly.

Direct to Solace is a coordination relationship. SOLACE sources, schedules, vets, and follows through. The hands-on work — trades, deliveries, services — is performed by licensed and insured Flathead Valley specialists on the household's behalf.

The retainer covers SOLACE's coordination, year-round. Vendor costs, materials, gratuities, travel, and any third-party fees pass through to the household, quoted in advance and billed transparently. Never marked up.

Confidentiality is the standard. No client is discussed outside the engagement. No third-party referrals are made without explicit consent. Records are kept privately and destroyed at the end of the relationship if asked.

Geographic coverage: based in the Flathead Valley. Travel and away-from-home coordination is offered on a best-effort basis in Year One, formalized in policy thereafter.
