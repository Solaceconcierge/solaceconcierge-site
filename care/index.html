How It Works

A standing arrangement, simply made.

  • $65 per request

    A simple per-request rate for the small, everyday acts. For families with regular needs, a monthly 4-pack at $225 holds the rhythm; additional tasks beyond four are arranged at $55 each. Pre-funded via secure invoice; receipts itemized.

  • The Cadence

    Requests are taken in advance whenever possible — a 24- to 48-hour lead time is standard, with rush arrangements available for the genuinely time-sensitive. We confirm, we follow up, we report.

  • Family Liaison, Optional

    For out-of-town family, quiet weekly summaries by email — birthdays remembered, anniversaries kept, the small notes that travel across distance.

  • The App, Coming Soon

    A private SOLACE app for ongoing requests and confirmations. Until launch, all coordination moves through email and direct conversation.

A Few Important Notes

SOLACE/Care is non-medical, by design.

SOLACE/Care does not provide nursing services, medication management, or any form of clinical or medical decision-making. We do not handle private funds beyond direct vendor coordination, and we do not make care decisions on behalf of a resident.

SOLACE/Care is independent — not affiliated with, employed by, or representing any care facility. We coordinate with facility staff as a respectful guest, on the resident's or family's behalf.

In keeping with Montana law, SOLACE/Care personnel are mandatory reporters under the Elder Abuse Prevention Act. Any suspected financial exploitation, abuse, or neglect will be reported to the appropriate authorities.
